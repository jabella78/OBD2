Privacy Policy - YaDiag Free & YaDiag Pro

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled by YaDiag Free (com.jabella78.yadiag.free) and YaDiag Pro (com.jabella78.yadiag.pro) across Apple iOS/iPadOS and Google Android platforms (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), developed by Jacopo Bellarosa.

1. Core Principles & Zero Tracking

User privacy is a core architectural principle of our software. The Application:

  • Does NOT collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, phone number, or IP address).
  • Does NOT use third-party advertising SDKs or analytics tracking frameworks.
  • Does NOT track user activity across other companies' apps or websites (No IDFA on iOS and no Advertising ID on Android, compliant with Apple App Tracking Transparency and Google Play Data Safety guidelines).

2. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) & Classic SPP Usage

The Application requests system permission to access Bluetooth exclusively to communicate locally with standard OBD2 diagnostic adapters (e.g., vLinker MC+/BM+/FD+, UniCarScan UCSI-2100, OBDLink CX/MX+) and transmit diagnostic frames over KWP2000 / UDS / ISO 15765-4 CAN-bus with the motorcycle's onboard ECUs.

  • On Android 12+ (API 31+), the BLUETOOTH_SCAN permission is strictly declared with android:usesPermissionFlags="neverForLocation".
  • Telemetry data exchanged via Bluetooth (engine RPM, vehicle speed, temperatures, Diagnostic Trouble Codes) remains strictly in the local memory of your iPhone or Android device.
  • No geographical location (GPS) or personal driving history data is ever derived, stored, or transmitted.

3. On-Device Local Storage (Keychain / Keystore & Preferences)

The Application stores on the user's device:

  • UserDefaults (iOS) / SharedPreferences (Android): User interface language preferences, units of measurement, and acknowledgment of safety disclaimers (Apple required reason code: CA92.1).
  • Apple Keychain (iOS) / Android Keystore & EncryptedSharedPreferences AES-256 (Android): If the user chooses to configure an optional API key for the AI Diagnostic Advisor (BYOK - Bring Your Own Key), that credential is encrypted and stored exclusively in the hardware-backed secure storage of the user's device. It is never synced or sent to developer servers.

4. AI Diagnostic Advisor & Anonymous Telemetry (Optional Features)

The AI Diagnostic Advisor is completely optional. Any telemetry data submitted for analysis includes only anonymous engine parameters and DTC fault codes (RPM, TPS, sensor voltages, ISC/O2 adaptation statuses). Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS directly to the official endpoint of the AI provider chosen by the user, only after explicit user confirmation. No personal user identifiers or vehicle VINs are attached.

5. User Rights, Data Deletion & Contact

Since the Application does not create user accounts or store personal data on external servers, all local preferences and API keys can be permanently erased at any time simply by uninstalling the Application or clearing app storage in iOS/Android settings.
