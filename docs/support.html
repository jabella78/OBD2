Frequently Asked Questions

Which Bluetooth adapters are supported?

YaDiag works with standard Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) OBD2 adapters. Recommended devices include:

  • vLinker MC+ Bluetooth 4.0 / BLE
  • UniCarScan UCSI-2100
  • OBDLink CX / MX+

How to connect to my motorcycle?

  1. Turn your motorcycle ignition ON (engine stopped for diagnostics).
  2. Plug your BLE adapter into the diagnostic connector using the appropriate 4-pin (Euro 4) or 16-pin (Euro 5) cable.
  3. Enable Bluetooth on your iPhone and launch YaDiag.
  4. Go to the Bluetooth tab and tap your adapter to connect.
